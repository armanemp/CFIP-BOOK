Data Architecture

Data products with lineage

CFIP treats data as governed products. Raw inputs remain recoverable; normalized records are versioned; derived features carry lineage; analytical outputs are reproducible.

Quality

Completeness, validity, freshness, uniqueness, consistency, anomaly rate and schema compatibility.
