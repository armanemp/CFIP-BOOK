Architecture Decision Records

Decisions that constrain the system

ADRs make architectural intent explicit. A decision is accepted only with context, alternatives, consequences, migration/rollback implications and evidence requirements.

ADR-001 — Python-first

Python 3.14 + FastAPI + Pydantic + SQLAlchemy 2 + Alembic. Modern typed backend; no legacy PHP/Laravel destination.

ADR-002 — Chart-first

Trading terminal is a single workspace; tools surround the chart rather than creating dashboard-heavy navigation.

ADR-003 — Hybrid search

Lexical and semantic retrieval are combined, then reranked, with quality measured on representative judgments.

ADR-004 — Governed autonomy

AI can research and sandbox changes; promotion and high-impact operations remain policy-controlled.

ADR-005 — Event backbone

NATS JetStream provides durable asynchronous workflows with versioned events, replay and DLQ handling.

ADR-006 — Provider abstraction

Search, LLM, market-data and payment providers sit behind explicit ports to limit lock-in and enable measured replacement.
