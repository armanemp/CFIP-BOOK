Billing & Entitlements

Crypto-only subscription lifecycle

The billing domain is a state machine, not a checkout button. Every transition is idempotent, auditable and reconcilable.

Product / Price→Checkout→Payment Intent→Address / Invoice→Verification→Settlement→Subscription→Entitlement

Idempotency

Every externally triggered mutation carries an idempotency key and durable outcome.

Ledger

Money movement and subscription state are separated from presentation state and reconciled against provider evidence.

Expiry

Entitlement activation and expiry are derived from authoritative subscription state, not client timestamps.

Reconciliation

Periodic jobs detect missing, duplicate, late or contradictory provider events and create reviewable cases.
