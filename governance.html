Governance

Policy, provenance and accountable autonomy

Governance is a runtime capability, not a document-only process. High-impact actions, AI changes, data access and production promotion are policy-controlled and auditable.

Change governance

ADR → implementation → automated evidence → review gates → deployment → post-release observation.

AI governance

Dataset provenance, model/version registry, evaluation suite, risk tier, approval state, canary metrics and rollback target.

Data governance

Classification, retention, lineage, quality, access policy, deletion semantics and immutable audit trail.

Proposal queue

Elyrava can generate mature proposals. Approval is explicit for governed changes; approved proposals become durable decisions and archived evidence.

Security governance

Least privilege, short-lived credentials, secret isolation, dependency review, signed artifacts and controlled administrative operations.

Accountability

Every governed action records actor, authority, target, decision, policy result, evidence references, timestamp and correlation identifiers.
